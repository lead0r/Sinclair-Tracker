Sinclair Tracker is an unofficial open-source companion for German John Sinclair audio dramas.

Features:
• 390 productions across Edition 2000, Classics, Specials, Tonstudio Braun and SINCLAIR
• daily checks for new episodes on the official series pages
• six selectable accent colours and a custom cross symbol
• listened status, ratings and personal notes
• search, filters, statistics and a random picker
• JSON backup and restore
• optional persistent offline covers
• links to official products and streaming providers
